# Employment

## Category Definition

Employment cases involve disputes or concerns related to a person's employment, workplace, wages, termination, employment conditions, or other work-related matters.

This category is used for legal-aid intake and triage classification.

AidRoute must not determine whether an employer's action is legally valid.

## Common Case Indicators

A case may belong to this category when the user mentions:

- Employer or workplace
- Job termination
- Dismissal
- Unpaid wages
- Salary dispute
- Workplace dispute
- Employment agreement
- Workplace harassment
- Working conditions
- Dispute regarding employment benefits

## Important Intake Information

The intake process may collect:

- Type of employment
- Employer relationship
- Date the issue occurred
- Employment status
- Relevant communications
- Amount or type of disputed payment
- Important deadlines
- Court or authority notices
- Relevant employment documents

## Document Indicators

Potentially relevant documents include:

- Employment agreement
- Termination letter
- Salary records
- Workplace notice
- Official correspondence
- Court or authority notice

## Urgency Indicators

Potential urgency indicators include:

- An imminent official deadline
- A scheduled hearing
- A deadline mentioned in an official notice
- A time-sensitive employment action

The presence of an indicator does not itself establish a legal conclusion.

## Human Review

Human review should be considered when:

- A deadline is detected
- The user reports an imminent employment action
- Important information is missing
- The category confidence is low
- The uploaded document cannot be reliably understood
- No approved guideline applies
