# Family

## Category Definition

Family cases involve legal-aid concerns related to family relationships, marriage, separation, maintenance, custody, domestic family disputes, or related family matters.

This category is used for legal-aid intake and triage classification.

AidRoute must not provide legal advice or determine legal rights.

## Common Case Indicators

A case may belong to this category when the user mentions:

- Marriage-related dispute
- Separation
- Divorce-related matter
- Maintenance
- Child custody
- Family dispute
- Child-related legal issue
- Domestic family conflict
- Family court matter

## Important Intake Information

The intake process may collect:

- Nature of the family dispute
- People involved
- Date of relevant events
- Whether children are involved
- Existing official proceedings
- Court dates
- Notices received
- Relevant documents
- Immediate safety or time-sensitive concerns when voluntarily reported

## Document Indicators

Potentially relevant documents include:

- Court notice
- Family court document
- Marriage-related document
- Official notice
- Existing order
- Relevant correspondence

## Urgency Indicators

Potential urgency indicators include:

- An imminent court date
- An official deadline
- A deadline mentioned in a document
- Immediate time-sensitive proceedings

The presence of an indicator does not itself establish a legal conclusion.

## Human Review

Human review should be considered when:

- The matter appears time-sensitive
- A court date is detected
- A document contains an important deadline
- The case is ambiguous
- Category confidence is low
- Approved information is unavailable
