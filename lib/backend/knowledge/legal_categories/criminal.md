# Criminal

## Category Definition

Criminal cases involve situations where the user reports an interaction with criminal law enforcement, an alleged offence, criminal proceedings, arrest, detention, or a criminal court matter.

This category is used for legal-aid intake and triage classification.

AidRoute must not determine whether an offence occurred or provide legal advice.

## Common Case Indicators

A case may belong to this category when the user mentions:

- Police
- Arrest
- Detention
- Criminal complaint
- FIR
- Criminal investigation
- Criminal court
- Accusation of an offence
- Summons or notice related to a criminal matter

## Important Intake Information

The intake process may collect:

- Nature of the reported issue
- Whether police or authorities are involved
- Whether the user has been arrested or detained
- Relevant dates
- Court dates
- Official notices
- Relevant documents
- Current status of the matter

## Document Indicators

Potentially relevant documents include:

- Police-related document
- FIR-related document
- Court summons
- Court notice
- Bail-related document
- Official correspondence

## Urgency Indicators

Potential urgency indicators include:

- Current detention
- Imminent court date
- Official deadline
- Time-sensitive police or court notice
- Deadline identified in an official document

The presence of an indicator does not itself establish a legal conclusion.

## Human Review

Human review should be considered when:

- The user reports current detention
- A court date is imminent
- An official deadline is detected
- The situation is unclear
- Category confidence is low
- Approved information is unavailable
