# Housing and Eviction

## Category Definition

Housing and eviction cases involve disputes related to residential accommodation, tenancy, eviction notices, possession of a property, rent-related issues, or threats to remove a person from their residence.

This category is used for legal-aid intake and triage classification.

AidRoute must not determine the legal validity of an eviction, tenancy agreement, or notice.

## Common Case Indicators

A case may belong to this category when the user mentions:

- Landlord or tenant
- Eviction or removal from a home
- Notice to vacate
- Rent-related dispute
- Threat of eviction
- Possession of residential property
- Tenancy or rental agreement
- Dispute regarding a rented home
- Immediate loss of housing

## Important Intake Information

The intake process may collect:

- Type of housing
- Whether the user currently lives at the property
- Relationship with the landlord or property owner
- Date the issue occurred
- Date a notice was received
- Deadline mentioned in a document
- Current housing status
- Relevant documents
- Whether the user has received any court or official notice

## Document Indicators

Potentially relevant documents include:

- Eviction notice
- Notice to vacate
- Rental agreement
- Rent-related notice
- Court notice
- Property-related correspondence

## Urgency Indicators

Potential urgency indicators include:

- An imminent deadline
- A date requiring the user to vacate
- A court date
- An official notice requiring action
- Immediate risk of losing housing
- A deadline identified in an uploaded document

The presence of an indicator does not itself establish a legal conclusion.

## Human Review

Human review should be considered when:

- The case appears time-sensitive
- Important dates are detected
- The uploaded document is unclear
- The category confidence is low
- The system cannot find an approved guideline
- The user's situation is ambiguous
