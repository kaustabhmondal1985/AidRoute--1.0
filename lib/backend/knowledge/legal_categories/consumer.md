# Consumer

## Category Definition

Consumer cases involve disputes between consumers and businesses, sellers, service providers, or other providers of goods and services.

This category is used for legal-aid intake and triage classification.

AidRoute must not determine whether a consumer has a valid legal claim.

## Common Case Indicators

A case may belong to this category when the user mentions:

- Purchase of goods
- Service provider dispute
- Defective product
- Payment dispute
- Refund dispute
- Warranty issue
- Online purchase problem
- Business or seller complaint
- Consumer complaint

## Important Intake Information

The intake process may collect:

- Product or service involved
- Seller or service provider
- Date of purchase
- Date of dispute
- Amount involved
- Payment method
- Complaint already submitted
- Important deadlines
- Relevant documents

## Document Indicators

Potentially relevant documents include:

- Invoice
- Receipt
- Warranty document
- Payment record
- Complaint correspondence
- Service agreement
- Official notice

## Urgency Indicators

Potential urgency indicators include:

- An official deadline
- A scheduled hearing
- A time-sensitive notice
- A deadline identified in a relevant document

The presence of an indicator does not itself establish a legal conclusion.

## Human Review

Human review should be considered when:

- A deadline is detected
- The case involves an official proceeding
- Important information is missing
- Category confidence is low
- No approved guideline applies
