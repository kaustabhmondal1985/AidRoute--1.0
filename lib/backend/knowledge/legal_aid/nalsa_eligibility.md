# Legal Aid Eligibility Information

## Purpose

This document contains approved information related to eligibility for legal-aid services.

AidRoute must use only verified and approved eligibility information.

## Eligibility Information

Eligibility criteria must be populated from an approved and verified legal-aid source.

The system must not infer eligibility from incomplete information.

## Intake Information

When eligibility assessment is required, relevant information may include:

- User's stated circumstances
- Relevant category of legal matter
- Information required by the approved eligibility guideline
- Supporting documents when relevant

## Uncertain Eligibility

If available information is insufficient to determine whether an eligibility criterion is satisfied:

- Do not assume eligibility.
- Do not assume ineligibility.
- Mark the information as requiring human review.

## Human Review

Eligibility questions requiring legal or institutional judgment must be reviewed by authorized legal-aid staff.
